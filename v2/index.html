What is this?

The blue skeleton is MediaPipe's own monocular 3D (its weakest axis is depth). onnxruntime-web is initialized on WebGPU here so a 2D→3D lifting model can be dropped in to recover better depth. No public browser-ready lifter exists yet — you export one (e.g. SimpleBaseline3D from MMPose) to ONNX, host it, paste the URL. The lifted skeleton then renders in orange next to MediaPipe's for A/B.
