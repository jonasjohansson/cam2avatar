What's happening?

Your camera drives a 3D avatar in real time, entirely in the browser. MediaPipe tracks your body, hands and face; the avatar copies your pose.

The camera frame shows tracking quality: green = full body, amber = show your legs, red = step into frame. The avatar only moves when your full body is confidently in view — otherwise it holds still (tune with the confidence slider). Stand back so your whole body is visible, well-lit, for the best result.

Where do I get more?

Mixamo animations: sign in free at mixamo.com, pick any animation, download FBX (without skin), drop the file here.

VRM characters: make one in VRoid Studio, or grab free ones from VRoid Hub / Booth. Drop the .vrm here. Tip: just drag any .vrm or .fbx anywhere on the page.
